Leadership

Two people.
One vision.

Built on cooperation, trust and the belief that a music platform grows when its people grow together.

FOUNDER / PRESIDENT

Mohit Badoria

Mohit is the Founder & President of SARGAM. From the beginning of college, he had been thinking about creating a dedicated music platform where beginners could learn without hesitation, intermediate artists could sharpen their skills, and experienced musicians could improve further by teaching and collaborating. He wanted to create the platform he felt was missing — a place where people can learn, teach, perform and grow together. SARGAM was formed on 16 August 2026.

VICE-PRESIDENT

Manish Chandra Arya

Manish works alongside Mohit as Vice-President and is an important part of turning the vision into action. Both are cooperative and supportive with each other, sharing ideas, solving problems together and keeping the focus on the growth of SARGAM and its artists. Manish brings the same spirit of coordination, support and collaboration that SARGAM was built around.
